## Requesting Time Off

<Steps>
  <Step>
    Go to **Absences** → **"Request Time Off"**
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Fill in the form:
    - **Start Date** - When your time off begins
    - **End Date** - When you return to work
    - **Type** - Vacation, sick leave, personal day, etc.
    - **Reason** - Brief explanation (optional but recommended)
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Click **"Submit Request"**
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="info">
Your manager(s) will be notified and can approve or deny the request.
</Callout>

## Planning with Holidays

When holidays affect your absence period, Z8 can show holiday context directly in the planning view. Holiday names and assigned holiday calendars help you understand which days count against your balance.

Use the preview before submitting a request. It can help you check balance impact, schedule conflicts, and coverage risk before the request reaches a manager.

### Coverage in the preview

The preview compares your absence with your own published shifts and your organization's coverage rules:

- **Only the time you're away counts.** A partial-day absence affects only the hours it overlaps. For the rest of the shift you still count as working. A night shift that starts the evening before your absence and runs into it is included too. Each affected shift shows which part of it you'll miss.
- **Warnings show the exact interval.** When published staffing would drop below a rule's minimum while you're away, the preview shows the date, area, time, and how many staff would remain. If staffing changes during your shift, each interval is listed separately.
- **Incomplete evidence is marked as incomplete.** If no coverage rule applies to part of your affected work, or shifts or rules couldn't be read, the preview says so instead of showing an all-clear. Any warnings it did find stay visible.

<Callout type="warn">
Coverage is based only on published shifts and configured coverage rules. It doesn't consider colleagues' absences or unpublished schedule changes. When the preview shows no warning, that doesn't guarantee enough staffing or that your request will be approved.
</Callout>

All times are read in your organization's time zone, so everyone sees the same result.

## Checking Request Status

<Steps>
  <Step>
    Go to **Absences**
  </Step>
  <Step>
    View the **"My Requests"** tab
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Check the status indicators:
    - **Pending** - Awaiting manager approval
    - **Approved** - Approved by your manager
    - **Denied** - Not approved (reason included)
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Vacation Balance

Your vacation balance for the year is made up of:
- Your vacation allowance: the days per year of your organization's vacation policy, or a custom allowance your admin set for you
- Days carried over from the previous year, until they expire
- Adjustments by your admin or manager (bonus days, corrections)
- On a policy that grants vacation monthly or biweekly: the days accrued so far this year

Approved and pending vacation in the year is taken off the balance.

Go to **Absences** to see the balance: the days remaining out of your total, and the days used and pending.

### Joining or leaving during the year

In a year you join or leave, your allowance can be prorated: you get 1/12 of the yearly allowance for each full month you are employed, and once your six-month waiting period is complete it never drops below the statutory minimum, unless you leave by 30 June. The balance then explains it, for example "8 of 30 days, prorated: started 1 Oct 2026, 3 full employment months". A custom allowance is never prorated.

## Approvals (For Managers)

<Callout type="info">
If you're a manager, you have additional responsibilities and permissions.
</Callout>

### Accessing Approvals

<Steps>
  <Step>
    Go to **Approvals** from the main navigation
  </Step>
  <Step>
    You'll see two tabs:
    - **Absence Requests** - Time-off requests
    - **Time Corrections** - Time entry corrections
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Reviewing Requests

<Tabs items={['View Details', 'Approve', 'Deny']}>
  <Tab value="View Details">
    See dates, reason, and employee information before making a decision.
  </Tab>
  <Tab value="Approve">
    Grant the request - the employee will be notified immediately.
  </Tab>
  <Tab value="Deny">
    Reject with a reason - always provide an explanation to help the employee understand.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

### Best Practices for Managers

<Callout type="info" title="Manager Tips">
- **Review requests promptly** - Employees plan based on approvals
- **Be fair and consistent** - Apply the same standards to all team members
- **Provide reasons for denials** - Help employees understand decisions
- **Check team coverage** - Ensure adequate staffing before approving
- **Communicate** - Talk to employees about complex scheduling issues
</Callout>

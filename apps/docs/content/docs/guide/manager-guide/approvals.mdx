The approval inbox gives managers one place to review requests from their teams. Depending on organization settings, approvals can include absences, time corrections, shift requests, travel expenses, and other operational workflows.

## Review Flow

1. Open the **Approval Inbox**.
2. Filter by request type, status, or team when needed.
3. Open a request and review dates, comments, attachments, and policy context.
4. Approve, reject, or request changes with a clear decision note.

## Team Manager Fallback

If an employee belongs to multiple teams, approval routing can use the employee's primary team manager first. When no primary manager is available, Z8 can fall back to another eligible team manager according to the organization's approval setup.

## Travel Expense Reports

Expense reports are decided as a whole. Open **View submitted report and receipts** to see exactly what was submitted: every expense, its receipts, conversions, projects and calculated allowances. Then:

- **Approve** the report. If it contains explained missing receipts, accept each explanation first.
- **Return for changes** with a note. You can also comment on individual expenses. The employee corrects and resubmits the report, and earlier submissions stay readable.
- **Reject** with a reason. The decision is final.

If the employee withdraws the report or submits a new version, older inbox actions and cards stop working. An approved report that was not exported or reimbursed yet can be reopened from its report page with a reason. For an exported or reimbursed report, the employee submits an adjustment instead. You review the adjustment with its reason and signed difference, and you can open the report it corrects. An approved adjustment cannot be reopened, because the report it corrects was already exported or reimbursed. A further adjustment corrects it.

## Decision Quality

Use decision notes for rejected or changed requests. Clear notes reduce duplicate submissions and help employees understand what to correct.

A kiosk is a shared device, such as a tablet at the staff entrance, where employees clock in and out with a kiosk PIN instead of signing in. Each kiosk belongs to one location and accepts only the employees assigned to that location.

---

## Setting Up a Kiosk

Organization owners and admins manage kiosks in **Settings -> Kiosks**. Create a location first: every kiosk belongs to one.

1. Choose **Add kiosk**.
2. Enter a **Name** (for example "Staff entrance"), choose the **Location**, and check the **Time zone**. Clock events recorded at the kiosk use this zone.
3. Optionally switch on the **Who-is-in board**, which shows on the kiosk who is in or on a break, by first name and last initial.
4. Choose **Create kiosk**. Z8 shows a pairing code and a QR code.
5. On the kiosk device, scan the QR code, or open the kiosk page and enter the code. The code works once and expires after 10 minutes.

The device stays paired until you pair it again or revoke the kiosk. Changes to a kiosk apply at its next request.

| Action | Effect |
|--------|--------|
| **Pair again** | Issues a new pairing code. If a device is paired, its token is rotated: that device stops working at once and must be paired again with the new code. For a kiosk with offline mode on, Z8 warns you first: clockings it saved offline and has not sent yet are refused when it reconnects. |
| **Revoke** | The kiosk stops working at its next request and cannot be paired again. Create a new kiosk to replace it. |

## Who Can Clock at a Kiosk

An employee can clock at a kiosk when they are:

- an active employee of the organization,
- assigned to the kiosk's location (on the employee or on the location, in **Settings**), and
- set up with a kiosk PIN of 4 to 6 digits.

Employees with an account choose their own kiosk PIN in their security settings. Managers can create kiosk-only employees, who have no email address, and set, reset or unlock their PIN on the employee's page in **Settings -> Employees**.

After five wrong PINs in a row the employee's PIN is locked for 15 minutes. A kiosk also accepts at most 30 PIN attempts per minute, whoever enters them.

## Kiosk Badges

A kiosk badge is a printed QR code that identifies an employee at a kiosk instead of their PIN. It never signs anyone in to Z8, and the employee's kiosk PIN keeps working alongside it. A kiosk reads badges only when its badge scanner is switched on (see [Clocking with a Badge](#clocking-with-a-badge)).

Owners, admins and the employee's direct manager manage badges in the **Kiosk access** card on the employee's page in **Settings -> Employees**, one employee at a time. Only active employees, including kiosk-only employees, can receive a badge.

| Action | Effect |
|--------|--------|
| **Issue badge** | Creates the employee's badge and offers it as a PDF with their name, the organization and the QR code. An employee has at most one active badge, and it does not expire. |
| **Replace badge** | Creates a new badge and PDF. Every copy of the old badge stops working at once. |
| **Revoke badge** | Every copy of the badge stops working for good. The employee's kiosk PIN is not changed. |

<Callout type="warn" title="Save the PDF when you issue the badge">
Z8 keeps only a fingerprint of the badge, never the badge itself, so it cannot show or download the same badge again. Save the PDF (**Download PDF**) or print it (**Open to print**) before you choose **Done** or leave the page. You can reprint a saved PDF as often as you need. If the file or a printed badge is lost, replace the badge.
</Callout>

Whoever holds a copy of a badge can use it, so treat it like a key. Cut it out along the dashed line; it fits a standard card sleeve.

### Badges and Departures

A badge ends with the employee's departure. It keeps working until the departure's cutoff, the start of the day after the last working day in the organization's timezone, and stops working at that moment, even if Z8 has not finished processing the departure yet. Scheduling a departure does not end the badge early; if you move or cancel the departure before its cutoff, the badge follows the current date. The card then shows that the last badge ended with the departure.

A rehire never brings an old badge back: every copy of it stays invalid. To let a rehired employee use a badge again, issue a new one. Until the rehire, Z8 offers no new badge. The employee's kiosk PIN is not changed by a departure or a rehire.

## Clocking at a Kiosk

1. The employee taps their name on the kiosk's home screen.
2. They enter their kiosk PIN.
3. The kiosk shows their state and today's day total, and only the actions that fit: **Clock in**, or **Start break** and **Clock out**, or **Resume work** and **End day**. Ending the day while on a break ends the work at the start of the break.
4. When vehicle use is asked or expected for the employee, the kiosk asks for the vehicle and odometer reading before the action (see [Vehicles and Kilometres](/docs/guide/admin-guide/vehicles-and-kilometres)).
5. A confirmation shows the action and its time, then the kiosk returns to the home screen.

The kiosk keeps an employee's PIN, state and day total only for their turn. It does not show history, corrections, projects or work categories.

### Clocking with a Badge

A kiosk can read kiosk badges with a QR scanner attached to the device, the kind that types what it reads like a keyboard (often called a keyboard or "wedge" scanner, connected by USB or Bluetooth). Owners and admins switch this on per kiosk with **Badge scanner** in the kiosk's **Edit** dialog; it is off for every kiosk until you switch it on. The kiosks list shows "Badge scanner on" for those kiosks.

Set the scanner up as a keyboard that ends each scan with Enter (most scanners do by default). Badges use only digits and capital letters without Y and Z, so they read correctly whatever keyboard layout the scanner or the device uses.

With the badge scanner on:

1. The employee scans their badge at the kiosk's home screen. They do not tap their name or enter a PIN, and the scan alone records nothing.
2. The kiosk shows their state, today's day total and the actions that fit, as after a PIN.
3. They choose the action; vehicle questions and the confirmation follow as usual, and the kiosk returns to the home screen.

Things to know:

- **Only the home screen reads badges.** A scan during someone's turn, on a confirmation or while the kiosk checks an action is ignored: it cannot switch the employee or press anything. Typing a name into the search works as before.
- **Every action checks the badge again.** If the badge was replaced or revoked, the employee's departure took effect, they are no longer assigned to the kiosk's location, the kiosk was revoked or its badge scanner switched off since the scan, the action is refused and nothing is recorded.
- **Refused badges name nobody.** An unknown, replaced, revoked or foreign badge, or one of an employee who cannot clock at this kiosk, shows the same message. A code that is not a kiosk badge (a link, a product barcode) is never opened.
- **Badge clocking needs a connection.** Offline, the kiosk says so and records and saves nothing for the badge. The employee can tap their name and use their PIN; with offline mode on, that PIN clocking is saved on the kiosk as usual.
- **PIN and badge are independent.** Wrong badges never count toward an employee's PIN lockout. A kiosk accepts at most 60 badge requests per minute (a scan and its action are two).
- **A lost answer is checked, never repeated.** If the connection drops after the employee chose an action, the kiosk shows "Checking whether your action was recorded" and offers nothing else until Z8 answers, also after a reload. If the action was recorded, the kiosk shows its original time. If Z8 confirms nothing was recorded, the kiosk asks the employee to scan their badge again (or use their PIN) and choose the action again; it never sends the old action on its own.

## Offline Mode

<Callout type="info" title="Not Yet Available">
  Offline mode is not available yet. It needs your organization to use coordinated clock commands, which no organization does yet. Until then, a kiosk works only while it is online, and a kiosk without a connection says so and records nothing.
</Callout>

With offline mode, a kiosk keeps clocking when its connection drops, also after a reload or a restart of the device. Owners and admins switch it on per kiosk with **Offline mode** in the kiosk's **Edit** dialog; it is off by default.

### What employees see offline

- The kiosk shows a banner that it is offline and that clockings are saved on the kiosk.
- After the employee taps their name and enters their PIN, the kiosk shows their state **as of** the time it last heard from Z8, together with what was already saved on this kiosk since. It never shows a day total offline.
- All actions work as online, including ending the day while on a break and recording vehicle use.
- The confirmation says the clocking is **saved on this kiosk and not yet confirmed**.

### How offline clockings are confirmed

The kiosk cannot check a PIN offline, and it keeps nothing that could: it seals each entered PIN so that only Z8 can read it. When the connection is back, the kiosk sends its saved clockings in the order they happened. Z8 then checks each PIN against the employee's current kiosk PIN, applies the PIN lockout in the order the attempts happened, and records each clocking at the time it happened. Each clocking is recorded once, even when the kiosk has to send it again.

The kiosk applies its limit of 30 PIN attempts per minute itself while offline. If five saved PINs of the same employee turn out wrong within 15 minutes of arriving, Z8 checks that employee's further saved clockings only after those 15 minutes. The kiosk keeps them and sends them again, so nothing is lost.

### Time of offline clockings

The kiosk takes the time from Z8's clock at its last contact and counts on with a clock the device user cannot change. After a restart without a connection, or after the device was asleep, it uses the device clock instead, and the entry records which time source was used. Clockings are recorded in the time zone the kiosk had when the employee clocked. Z8 accepts offline clockings up to 7 days late.

### Refused offline clockings

Z8 refuses a saved clocking it cannot accept, for example a wrong or locked PIN, an employee no longer assigned to the location, or a time outside the accepted window. If a kiosk is revoked or paired again while offline, Z8 refuses all of its saved clockings.

For each refused clocking:

- the kiosk shows a short notice on its home screen;
- the employee gets a notification saying what was not recorded and why. For a kiosk-only employee, their managers get it instead;
- owners and admins see it under **Refused offline clockings** in **Settings -> Kiosks**, with the employee, the action, the time it claimed, the time source and the reason.

### What the kiosk keeps

An offline-mode kiosk keeps the kiosk's details, the assigned employees with their last known state and their vehicle-use details, and its saved clockings with their sealed PINs. It deletes what it no longer needs at its next contact: everything when offline mode is switched off or the kiosk is revoked, and an employee once they are no longer assigned to the location. Saved clockings are still sent and judged.

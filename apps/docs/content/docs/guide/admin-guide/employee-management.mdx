## Adding a New Employee

<Steps>
  <Step>
    Go to **Settings** → **Employees**
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Click **"Add Employee"** button
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Fill in the employee information:
    - **User Account**: Select existing user or invite new user
    - **First Name**: Employee's given name
    - **Last Name**: Employee's family name
    - **Gender**: Optional (Male, Female, Other)
    - **Birthday**: Optional, for birthday tracking
    - **Position**: Job title or role
    - **Team**: Assign to a team (optional)
    - **Role**: System role (Admin, Manager, Employee)
    - **Start Date**: Employment start date
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Click **"Create Employee"**
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="info" title="Important Notes">
- User accounts are separate from employee profiles
- Membership is managed per organization, so review the active organization's member record and invitation status when connecting a user to an employee profile
- Initial manager assignment can be done after creation
- New employees are active by default
</Callout>

## Editing Employee Information

<Steps>
  <Step>
    Go to **Settings** → **Employees**
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Find the employee (use search if needed)
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Click on their name or **"View Details"**
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Edit the information:
    - Personal information (name, gender, birthday)
    - Job information (position, team, role)
    - Status (active/inactive)
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Click **"Save Changes"**
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Members And Invitations

Organization membership is managed from **Settings** -> **Organization**. Use that area when you need to invite someone before their employee profile exists or when you need to manage pending invitations.

Admins and owners can:

- Send invitations to new members by email
- Cancel pending invitations
- Review current members for the active organization
- Adjust organization-level access before or after creating the employee record

Employee profiles and organization memberships are related, but they are not the same thing. Add or invite the person to the organization first when necessary, then create or connect the employee profile used for workforce records.

<Callout type="info" title="Organization scope">
  Invitation and membership changes apply only to the active organization.
</Callout>

## Deactivating Employees

When an employee leaves:

<Steps>
  <Step>
    Navigate to the employee's profile
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Change status to **"Inactive"**
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Click **"Save"**
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="warn" title="What happens when deactivated">
- Employee loses system access
- Cannot submit new requests
- Historical data remains intact
- Can be reactivated later if needed
</Callout>

<Callout type="info">
**Best Practice**: Keep employee records for at least one full year after departure for reporting and compliance purposes.
</Callout>

## Contract Types & Hourly Rates

Contract type and hourly wage are part of an employee's **employment terms**, on the **Contract & Work Model** card of the employee's profile. Only org admins can change them.

### Contract Type Selection

When you prepare an invited employee's profile, choose their contract type:

- **Fixed Salary**: Monthly or annual salary
- **Hourly**: Paid by the hour. Enter the hourly wage too.

When the invitation is accepted, an hourly employee with a wage starts with confirmed employment terms that carry that wage. Later contract type changes go through the **Contract & Work Model** card. The employee edit form shows the current contract type but does not change it.

### Changing an Hourly Wage

<Steps>
  <Step>
    Open the employee's profile and find the **Contract & Work Model** card
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Click **Change wage**
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Enter the date the new wage applies from, the new wage, and a reason
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Save. The change is confirmed right away unless you save it as a draft.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The new terms copy the contract type, weekly hours, work model, work policy and currency from the terms in force on that date. To change anything else, add a full change on the card instead.

<Callout type="info">
  A wage change takes effect at local midnight of its date. Draft and pending terms never affect earnings. Only confirmed hourly terms do. A shift that crosses midnight on the change date is paid at the old wage before midnight and the new wage after it.
</Callout>

### Wage History

The **Contract & Work Model** card's timeline is the employee's wage history. Every set of terms shows its dates, its wage and the reason for the change. Employee reports compute earnings from each stretch of confirmed hourly terms in the report period. Work under fixed terms earns nothing in that report.

### Wage Best Practices

- **Date changes correctly** - A wage applies from its date until the next confirmed terms
- **Give a reason** - The reason is stored with the terms and shown in the timeline
- **Review regularly** - Ensure wages stay current with agreements

## Adding a New Employee

<Steps>
  <Step>
    Go to **Settings** → **Employees**
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Click **"Add Employee"** button
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Fill in the employee information:
    - **User Account**: Select existing user or invite new user
    - **First Name**: Employee's given name
    - **Last Name**: Employee's family name
    - **Gender**: Optional (Male, Female, Other)
    - **Birthday**: Optional, for birthday tracking
    - **Position**: Job title or role
    - **Team**: Assign to a team (optional)
    - **Role**: System role (Admin, Manager, Employee)
    - **Start Date**: Employment start date
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Click **"Create Employee"**
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="info" title="Important Notes">
- User accounts are separate from employee profiles
- Membership is managed per organization, so review the active organization's member record and invitation status when connecting a user to an employee profile
- Initial manager assignment can be done after creation
- New employees are active by default
</Callout>

## Editing Employee Information

<Steps>
  <Step>
    Go to **Settings** → **Employees**
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Find the employee (use search if needed)
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Click on their name or **"View Details"**
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Edit the information:
    - Personal information (name, gender, birthday)
    - Job information (position, team, role)
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Click **"Save Changes"**
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Members And Invitations

Organization membership is managed from **Settings** -> **Organization**. Use that area when you need to invite someone before their employee profile exists or when you need to manage pending invitations.

Admins and owners can:

- Send invitations to new members by email
- Cancel pending invitations
- Review current members for the active organization
- Adjust organization-level access before or after creating the employee record

Employee profiles and organization memberships are related, but they are not the same thing. Add or invite the person to the organization first when necessary, then create or connect the employee profile used for workforce records.

<Callout type="info" title="Organization scope">
  Invitation and membership changes apply only to the active organization.
</Callout>

## Deactivating Employees

When an employee leaves:

<Steps>
  <Step>
    Go to **Settings** → **Employees** and find the employee
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Open the row's actions menu, or the employee's profile, and choose **Deactivate**
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Review what changes. If someone they manage would be left without a manager, choose a new manager for them (a current admin or manager)
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Confirm
  </Step>
</Steps>

Only owners and admins can deactivate, and the employee's details form doesn't change whether someone is active.

Before you confirm, the dialog lists everything the deactivation changes. Z8 checks it again when you confirm: if a new manager you chose can no longer manage the report, the dialog says so and you can choose someone else. Confirming twice, for example after a lost connection, never deactivates twice.

<Callout type="warn" title="What happens when deactivated">
- Employee loses system access and is signed out
- They become a **former employee**: they no longer appear where Z8 shows who works here now, and are shown as *Former since* the day you deactivated them
- **Managers**: the people they managed keep their other manager, or get the new manager you chose. People they managed who have left themselves just lose the link. Their own manager links end
- **Teams and projects**: they leave their teams, their own project assignments and their project manager roles
- **Approvals**: requests waiting for their decision move to the new manager you chose for that person, or to the next approver the workflow finds. Anything that can't move appears on their employee page under *Needs review*
- **Shifts**: upcoming shifts become open shifts, and their current managers get one notice (or, if they had none, the organization's owners and admins). Past shifts and a shift already running stay
- **Absences**: pending and approved absences after their last day are cancelled, which frees the allowance. An absence running over their last day stays, and so does one in a closed month (reopen the month to cancel it). They stop covering other people's absences as deputy
- **Access**: expense officer, payroll access, personnel file officer and driving licence verifier roles end, driving authorizations end, kiosk badges and their report share links stop working
- Personnel file retention counts from the day you deactivated them
- They stop counting as a paid seat
- Historical data remains intact
- Can be reactivated later if needed; **Reactivate** counts them as a seat again, but restores none of the above
</Callout>

### Reactivating employees

Choose **Reactivate** in the row's actions menu or on the employee's profile. The employee can sign in again, is a current employee again and counts as a paid seat. Personnel file retention stops counting.

Reactivation restores nothing the deactivation ended. Right after you reactivate, Z8 shows **What to grant again**: the manager links and moved reports, roles, kiosk badges, driving authorizations, teams and projects, opened shifts, cancelled absences and revoked report share links of that deactivation. Each item links to the page where you grant it again, so you only give back what the person still needs. The list stays on the employee's profile until an admin dismisses it. The deactivation's open *Needs review* items close, because the person is a current employee again.

The employee's **Contract & Work Model** card lists each deactivation and reactivation with its date and time in your organization's time zone. Deactivations from before this list existed appear while their audit log entries are kept (one year).

### Current and former employees

A former employee is someone who was deactivated, or whose departure has taken effect. Z8 keeps their past work, absences, decisions and documents, but leaves them out of everything about the present:

- **Team views**: the dashboard's *Your team*, *Who's out today* and *Upcoming time off*, the team page, team counts and averages, chat-bot team commands and manager digests show current employees only.
- **Lists you manage**: the team page, team members and project members hide former employees. Turn on **Show former employees** to see them, marked *Former*.
- **Employee directory**: **Settings** → **Employees** opens on **Current**, which also lists pending invitations. Choose **Former**, **Draft** or **All** to see the others.
- **Pickers**: choosing someone for a team, a manager, a shift, a policy, a vehicle, a grant or a document request offers current employees only. Z8 also refuses to save a former employee into such a role, while removing one still works.
- **Notifications**: manager notices skip former managers. A request that needs a decision goes to the organization admins when every manager of the employee has left.
- **Views of the past**: reports, past calendars and the team calendar feed keep former employees' past entries, marked *Former*; their later absences are left out.

<Callout type="info">
**Best Practice**: Keep employee records for at least one full year after departure for reporting and compliance purposes.
</Callout>

## Contract Types & Hourly Rates

Contract type and hourly wage are part of an employee's **employment terms**, on the **Contract & Work Model** card of the employee's profile. Only org admins can change them.

### Contract Type Selection

When you prepare an invited employee's profile, choose their contract type:

- **Fixed Salary**: Monthly or annual salary
- **Hourly**: Paid by the hour. Enter the hourly wage too.

When the invitation is accepted, an hourly employee with a wage starts with confirmed employment terms that carry that wage. Later contract type changes go through the **Contract & Work Model** card. The employee edit form shows the current contract type but does not change it.

### Changing an Hourly Wage

<Steps>
  <Step>
    Open the employee's profile and find the **Contract & Work Model** card
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Click **Change wage**
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Enter the date the new wage applies from, the new wage, and a reason
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Save. The change is confirmed right away unless you save it as a draft.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The new terms copy the contract type, weekly hours, work model, work policy and currency from the terms in force on that date. To change anything else, add a full change on the card instead.

<Callout type="info">
  A wage change takes effect at local midnight of its date. Draft and pending terms never affect earnings. Only confirmed hourly terms do. A shift that crosses midnight on the change date is paid at the old wage before midnight and the new wage after it.
</Callout>

### Wage History

The **Contract & Work Model** card's timeline is the employee's wage history. Every set of terms shows its dates, its wage and the reason for the change. Employee reports compute earnings from each stretch of confirmed hourly terms in the report period. Work under fixed terms earns nothing in that report.

### Wage Best Practices

- **Date changes correctly** - A wage applies from its date until the next confirmed terms
- **Give a reason** - The reason is stored with the terms and shown in the timeline
- **Review regularly** - Ensure wages stay current with agreements

Z8 controls who can access an organization through its enterprise identity setup: SSO enforcement, domain and invite restrictions, and the role template applied to provisioned users. Each user also manages their own sign-in security.

<Callout type="info" title="Not Yet Available">
  Z8 does not offer IP, location, device, or session access policies. Organizations cannot restrict access by IP address or country, require trusted or managed devices, require MFA or passkeys for all members, or set session duration, idle timeout, or concurrent session limits.
</Callout>

---

## Organization Access Policy

Organization owners and admins configure the access policy in **Settings -> Enterprise -> Enterprise Identity Setup**, in the **Access Policy** step.

1. Complete the **Provider**, **Domain**, **SSO Configuration**, and **Test User** steps first. The **SCIM Provisioning** step is optional.
2. In **Access Policy**, choose the rules that apply to your organization:

   | Option | Effect once activated |
   |--------|-----------------------|
   | **Require SSO** | Members can only open the organization in a session that was signed in through the organization's SSO provider. A session started any other way must sign in through the identity provider first. |
   | **Restrict to verified domain** | Invite codes can only be redeemed by users whose email address belongs to the enterprise identity domain. |
   | **Restrict invites** | Email invitations can only be sent to addresses in the enterprise identity domain. |

3. Optionally select a **Default role template** (see below).
4. Choose **Save access policy**.
5. In **Review & Activate**, choose **Activate enterprise identity**.

The rules take effect only after activation. **Require SSO** also needs the enterprise identity domain to be added and verified in **Settings -> Enterprise -> Domain & Branding**; activation fails without it.

<Callout type="warn" title="Avoid Locking Yourself Out">
  Before activating **Require SSO**, confirm that your own admin account can sign in through the configured identity provider.
</Callout>

## Role Templates

A role template defines the employee role and organization-level team permissions given to a user who is provisioned through SCIM.

### Default Role Template

Choose the **Default role template** in the **Access Policy** step. The list shows the active role templates owned by your organization and the global templates available to all organizations. Select **No default template** to clear it.

A SCIM connection cannot be created until a default role template is selected. See [SCIM Provisioning](/docs/guide/admin-guide/scim-provisioning).

### How SCIM Applies a Template

When SCIM provisions or updates a user, Z8:

1. Uses the role template mapped to the user's SCIM Group `externalId`, if one exists, and otherwise the connection's default role template.
2. Sets the user's employee role and organization-level team permissions from the template, and adds the template's default team if it has one.
3. Records a `role_template_applied` provisioning event whenever the applied template changes.

Z8 does not overwrite a role that was assigned manually, through SSO, or through an invite code.

<Callout type="info" title="No Template Editor">
  Z8 has no settings page for creating role templates or group-to-template mappings. Choose from the templates already available to your organization.
</Callout>

## Personal Security Settings

Each user manages their own sign-in security in **Settings -> Security**. These are personal settings, not organization policies.

| Section | What users can do |
|---------|-------------------|
| **Change Password** | Update their password |
| **Two-Factor Authentication** | Enable or disable two-factor authentication for their account |
| **Passkeys** | Add and delete passkeys for passwordless sign-in |
| **Social Accounts** | Connect and disconnect the social sign-in providers enabled for the deployment |
| **Active Sessions** | Review their sessions, revoke a single session, or choose **Revoke All Other Sessions** |

## Troubleshooting

### A member cannot open the organization

**Require SSO** is active and the member's current session was not signed in through the organization's SSO provider. They need to complete sign-in with the identity provider.

### Activation fails with Require SSO selected

In **Settings -> Enterprise -> Domain & Branding**, add the enterprise identity domain with **Add Custom Domain** and verify it with **Verify**. Then activate again.

### An invitation or invite code is rejected

**Restrict invites** or **Restrict to verified domain** is active. Use an email address in the enterprise identity domain, or change the access policy.

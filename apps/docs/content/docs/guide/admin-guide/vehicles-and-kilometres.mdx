Employees can record the vehicle they drove during a work period, with a start and an end odometer reading. Z8 derives the driven distance in whole kilometres. Organization admins keep a list of organization vehicles and decide who is asked for vehicle use. Managers see work that lacks driving details, and everyone can report the distance per vehicle.

<Callout type="warn" title="Not a tax logbook">
Z8 is not a tax-compliant logbook (Fahrtenbuch). Readings can be corrected after the fact, and Z8 records no trip purpose, business partner or route. A work period has at most one vehicle, so Z8 does not record separate trips. If you need a logbook for tax purposes, keep it in a dedicated tool.
</Callout>

## Organization vehicles

Organization owners and admins manage vehicles in **Settings → Vehicles**.

| Field | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Name | Up to 100 characters, for example "Service van 3". |
| Licence plate | Up to 20 characters and unique in your organization. Spaces, hyphens and upper or lower case don't make a plate different. |
| Assigned teams | Every member of an assigned team gets this vehicle suggested first. |
| Assigned employees | These employees get this vehicle suggested first. |

Assigned drivers only change the order of the vehicle picker. Anyone who records vehicle use can pick any active vehicle. Every employee can also pick **Own vehicle**, their private vehicle, which Z8 records without identifying it.

**Archiving** hides a vehicle from the pickers. It stays on the work it was recorded on, in reports and in exports, and you can restore it. **Deleting** is possible only while the vehicle was never recorded on work, and it removes its assigned drivers. Otherwise Z8 refuses the delete and offers to archive the vehicle instead.

Creating, changing, archiving, restoring and deleting a vehicle, and changing its assigned drivers, are recorded in the audit log. Vehicles belong to one organization, and no other organization can see them.

## Who is asked

**Settings → Vehicle use** controls who records vehicle use.

1. Switch on **Record vehicle use**. It is off by default. Switching it on applies to nobody until you add an assignment.
2. Add assignments for the whole organization, a team or an employee, each with a mode:
   - **Asked**: the employee sees optional vehicle and reading inputs when they clock.
   - **Vehicle use expected**: the employee is asked every time, and their completed work is flagged as **driving details missing** until it has a vehicle and its readings.
   - **Off (excluded)**: excludes a team or an employee from a broader assignment.

The most specific assignment wins: an employee's own assignment over their primary team's, and a team's over the whole organization's. Changes to the switch and the assignments are recorded in the audit log.

Nobody is ever stopped or delayed from clocking because vehicle use is missing, in any mode. Switching vehicle use off stops asking and stops flagging driving details missing. Vehicle use that was already recorded stays visible, read-only, and in exports.

Only work that started after an employee became expected to record vehicle use is flagged. Turning the expectation on never flags work recorded earlier.

## Recording vehicle use

Vehicle use is part of a work period's attribution, like its project or work location:

- **Vehicle**: one organization vehicle or the employee's own vehicle.
- **Start and end reading**: the odometer in whole kilometres. An end reading below the start reading is refused.
- **Driven distance**: the end reading minus the start reading. When there is no start reading, the employee can enter the distance directly.

When an employee picks an organization vehicle, the start reading is pre-filled with that vehicle's last recorded end reading. The vehicle assigned to them is pre-selected.

Z8 shows a warning, but never refuses, when:

- the start reading is below the vehicle's last recorded end reading,
- the vehicle is already on another employee's running work,
- the distance implies an average speed above 150 km/h over the work period, or
- the distance is more than 1,500 km in one work period.

The same warnings are shown to the approver of a correction.

### Clocking

With vehicle use asked or expected, the clock asks for it:

- **Clock-in** asks for the vehicle and the start reading.
- **Clock-out** asks for the end reading, or for the driven distance when there is no start reading.
- **A break** carries the vehicle over to the work after the break and asks for one optional reading. That reading is both the end reading of the work before the break and the start reading of the work after it. If it is skipped, both stay empty.
- While clocked in, the employee can change the vehicle or the start reading of their running work.

The web clock (the clock popover, the clock widget and the quick break) asks for vehicle use. So do the installable web app, also offline, and the mobile app, which use the same clock. Offline, the vehicle list is the one last loaded, and the warnings appear only with a connection. If entered vehicle use cannot be recorded, the clock event still goes ahead, and the employee is told that the vehicle use was not recorded.

The kiosk asks after the employee has entered their PIN, and it asks for the break reading when the employee comes back from the break. The desktop app and the Teams bot ask for vehicle use too. The Teams bot asks at clock-in and clock-out only, because it has no break command.

Automatic clock-outs, clock-outs at an employee's departure and clock-outs on someone else's behalf leave the end reading empty. With vehicle use expected, such work then shows driving details missing until the employee adds the reading.

### Corrections

Vehicle use can be added or changed afterwards:

- in the work period dialog in the **Calendar**,
- when entering work manually, and
- when splitting a work period in the calendar. Both halves keep the vehicle. The split asks for one optional value: the odometer reading at the split when the work has readings, or else the kilometres driven in the first half. The rest goes to the second half. If it is skipped, the whole distance stays on the first half.

An employee's changes to their own completed work follow the same [change policy](/docs/guide/admin-guide/change-policies) as the work location: direct within the self-service window, then by approval, then refused. Admins change it directly. A closed month freezes vehicle use like every other part of the work. Changing it sends a submitted period back. When someone else changed it, the work period shows "(edited by NAME at …)", and a time-correction approval shows the vehicle and readings before and after.

Historical and gap repairs keep vehicle use as it is and never invent readings.

## Driving details missing

Completed work of an employee with vehicle use expected that lacks its vehicle or a reading shows **driving details missing** until it is complete. Running work is not flagged until it ends.

- **Employees** see a "Driving details missing" notice on **Time Tracking** that lists their flagged work periods, each with a link to the calendar. Each flagged period is also marked in the history table.
- **Employees** get one reminder on the morning after the work, at 08:00 in their timezone, through the clocking reminders. It is sent at most once. They can turn it off under **Driving details missing** in their notification settings.
- **Managers** see "Driving details missing" with a count in their daily briefing on **Today**. It opens a list of the flagged work of the employees they manage, which they can filter by team and employee.
- **Approvers** of a period submission see flagged work as a warning. It never blocks the approval.

## Distance per vehicle

**Reports → Distance per Vehicle** (`/reports/vehicles`) shows the kilometres per vehicle and per employee for each month of a chosen period. It counts the whole kilometres of completed work in the month the work started, at the local time where it was recorded. Own-vehicle driving is listed as **Own vehicle**.

| Who | What they see |
| --- | --- |
| Organization owners and admins | All vehicles and employees |
| Managers | Themselves and the employees they manage |
| Employees | Their own driving |

The report does not depend on the vehicle use switch, so earlier driving stays reportable after vehicle use is switched off.

## Exports and API

Vehicle use is visible to whoever may see the work period's attribution.

- The CSV `work_periods` data export, and the scheduled data exports that reuse it, end with the columns `vehicleKind` (`organization` or `own`), `vehicleName`, `licenceNumber`, `odometerStartKm`, `odometerEndKm` and `drivenDistanceKm`. The earlier columns keep their order. An own vehicle has no name or plate.
- [`GET /api/v1/work-periods`](/docs/api-reference/listWorkPeriods) and the [MCP tool](/docs/api-reference/mcp) `list_work_periods` return a nullable `vehicleUse` object with `vehicleKind`, `vehicleId`, `vehicleName`, `licencePlate`, `odometerStartKm`, `odometerEndKm` and `drivenDistanceKm`, under the existing `time-entries:read` scope. Archived vehicles keep their name.
- Payroll exports are unchanged and contain no distances.

## Own-vehicle mileage

Z8 does not reimburse driven distance by itself. Pricing, approval and reimbursement stay in [Travel Expenses](/docs/guide/admin-guide/travel-expenses).

On a completed work period driven in an own vehicle, the employee can choose **Add to expense report** in the calendar's work period dialog. They pick one of their draft trip reports or a new mileage report. Z8 adds a mileage expense pre-filled with the work period's local start date, the driven distance and the vehicle kind (car by default, changeable). The employee enters the route.

Each work period's distance can be claimed once. After it is added, the work period shows that it is in an expense report. If that mileage expense is deleted, the work period can be added again. Organization vehicles are not claimed this way.

## Works council

When your organization uses the works council portal, its dashboard and its CSV review pack include a vehicle use section. It shows the switch, the assignments, their modes and the number of vehicles, following the portal's identity and aggregation rules. It never shows readings or distances per employee.

<Callout type="info">
Not included: a tax-compliant logbook, trips or several vehicles in one work period, distances from GPS or position stamps, miles or decimal kilometres, special consent or retention for vehicle use, and fleet management such as maintenance, fuel, costs or bookings.
</Callout>

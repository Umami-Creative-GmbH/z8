## Platform Support

The z8 Timer desktop app is available for **Windows only**. On other operating systems, use the Z8 web app in your browser.

| Platform | Installer | Notes |
|----------|-----------|-------|
| **Windows** | `.msi`, `.exe` | Supports Windows 10 and newer systems, with WebView2 required for the app shell. |

# Absences

Employees request time away from work, such as vacation or sick leave, and managers approve it. Absences draws the vacation each employee takes from their allowance.

## Language

### Counting

**Working day**:
A day the employee's work policy in effect on that day schedules them to work, and that isn't a public holiday for them. An employee whose policy has no schedule works Monday to Friday.
_Avoid_: business day, weekday, workday

**Absence days**:
How many working days an absence takes. Each working day counts 1, and a morning or afternoon half counts ½, however many hours the day has.
_Avoid_: business days, vacation hours, calendar days

### Vacation

**Vacation allowance**:
The absence days an employee may take as vacation in a year, before carryover and adjustments. For a part-time employee it is already reduced to their working days, because absence days count only the days they work.
_Avoid_: vacation entitlement, annual hours

**Vacation balance**:
An employee's vacation allowance plus carryover and adjustments, minus the absence days of their approved and pending vacation in that year.
_Avoid_: remaining vacation, leave account

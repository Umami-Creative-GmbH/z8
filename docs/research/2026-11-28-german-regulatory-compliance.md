- **Date:** 2026-11-28
- **Scope:** Z8 as a time tracking and workforce management product for German customers (time recording, breaks, shifts, absences, sick notes, travel expenses, personnel file, payroll export, statutory monitors, audit exports). ### F-01 — § 8 Abs. 1 Nr. 2 SGB IV: the short-term monitor applies the general 70-day / 3-month limits to agricultural establishments (90 working days / 15 weeks since 2026-01-01)

- **Classification:** Rule drift / edge case
- **Legal basis and sources**
  - § 8 Abs. 1 Nr. 2 SGB IV, second half-sentence, as consolidated today: „bei einer Beschäftigung in einem landwirtschaftlichen Betrieb gilt eine zeitliche Grenze von 15 Wochen oder 90 Arbeitstagen." https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/sgb_4/__8.html
  - Amending law: Gesetz zur Anpassung des Sechsten Buches Sozialgesetzbuch und anderer Gesetze, BGBl. 2025 I Nr. 355, issued 23.12.2025. https://www.recht.bund.de/bgbl/1/2025/355/VO.html
  - Effective 1 January 2026: Deutsche Rentenversicherung, „Mit dem SGB VI-Anpassungsgesetz wurde zum 1. Januar 2026 eingeführt, dass bei einer Beschäftigung in einem landwirtschaftlichen Betrieb eine zeitliche Grenze von 15 Wochen oder 90 Arbeitstagen gilt." https://www.deutsche-rentenversicherung.de/DRV/DE/Experten/Arbeitgeber-und-Steuerberater/summa-summarum/Lexikon/K/kurzfristige_beschaeftigung.html
  - Geringfügigkeits-Richtlinien of 5 January 2026, B 2.3.4: scope is the Klassifikation der Wirtschaftszweige (2025), Abschnitt A, Abteilung 01, Gruppen 01.1–01.6; mixed businesses count by where most employees work; when several stints are added up, 15 weeks count as 105 calendar days. https://www.minijob-zentrale.de/SharedDocs/Downloads/DE/Rundschreiben/Geringfuegigkeitsrichtlinien-2026.pdf?__blob=publicationFile
  - Same content for employers: https://magazin.minijob-zentrale.de/landwirtschaft-kurzfristige-minijobs-2026/
- **Verified codebase gap**
  - `apps/webapp/drizzle/0333_statutory_foundation.sql:35,40` seeds `de_short_term_max_work_days_per_year = 70` and `de_short_term_max_months_per_year = 3` open-ended from 2021-11-01, both referenced to § 8 Abs. 1 Nr. 2 SGB IV. No agricultural value exists.
  - `apps/webapp/src/lib/statutory/threshold-catalog.ts` has only those two short-term keys. `apps/webapp/src/lib/statutory/short-term/load-inputs.ts:45` knows the limit kinds `work_days` and `months` only; `judge.ts:684-685` reads only the two general keys, and `judge.ts:600` computes the limit for added-up stints as the month limit × 30 calendar days.
  - No field marks an organization or employment as agricultural: `grep -iE "industry|sector|branche|wirtschaftszweig"` over `apps/webapp/src/db/schema/*.ts` returns nothing. `grep -iE "landwirt|agricultur"` over `apps/webapp/src` hits only JArbSchG sector text (`statutory/youth/jarbschg-rules.ts`, youth warning components).
- **Product impact:** For an agricultural establishment the short-term monitor turns warning and critical 20 working days (or about two weeks) too early and reports a breach where the law allows the employment. Required: effective-dated agricultural baselines (90 working days, 15 weeks, 105 calendar days for added-up stints) from 2026-01-01 and week-based counting beside the month-based one. Eligibility is decided per employing establishment (Beschäftigungsbetrieb, identified by its Betriebsnummer): WZ 2025 groups 01.1–01.6, and for a mixed establishment the area where most of its employees work. A tenant-wide switch would misclassify organizations with several establishments, so the declaration has to sit on the establishment or the employment, made by an org admin. The separate Berufsmäßigkeit test stays as it is.
- **Dedup:** searches `agriculture short-term`, `90 Arbeitstage`, `landwirtschaft` (issues and PRs): no match. #1990 (closed, the short-term monitor) specifies 70 days / 3 months only; its body contains neither "landwirt", "agricult" nor "90".
- **Confidence:** high on the law. Open doubt: whether Z8 has agricultural customers at all, which decides priority, not correctness.
- **Validation (Astra 6.2 High):** `REVISE`, applied. Law and gap confirmed (§ 8 Abs. 1 Nr. 2 SGB IV; Geringfügigkeits-Richtlinien B 2.3.4, pp. 48–49). Corrections: eligibility per employing establishment instead of a tenant-wide flag; code references corrected (`judge.ts:684-685` reads the two general keys, `judge.ts:600` computes the added-up limit as the month limit × 30; line 78 is a comment). Dedup: #1990 and #1988 cover the general limits only.

### F-02 — § 7 Abs. 3 BUrlG: automatic carryover cap and expiry can understate legally outstanding leave (employer notice, long illness)

- **Classification:** Current gap
- **Legal basis and sources**
  - § 7 Abs. 3 BUrlG (leave must be taken in the calendar year; carried-over leave within the first three months of the next). https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/burlg/__7.html
  - CJEU 6 November 2018, C-684/16 (Max-Planck-Gesellschaft), para. 45: the employer must ensure the worker is actually able to take the leave, „indem er ihn – erforderlichenfalls förmlich – auffordert, dies zu tun, und ihm … klar und rechtzeitig mitteilt", that the leave will otherwise lapse. Official text, CELEX 62016CJ0684: http://publications.europa.eu/resource/celex/62016CJ0684
  - BAG 19 February 2019, 9 AZR 423/16, headnote 1: „Der Anspruch auf den gesetzlichen Mindesturlaub (§§ 1, 3 Abs. 1 BUrlG) erlischt … nur dann am Ende des Kalenderjahres (§ 7 Abs. 3 Satz 1 BUrlG) oder eines zulässigen Übertragungszeitraums …, wenn der Arbeitgeber den Arbeitnehmer zuvor in die Lage versetzt hat, seinen Urlaubsanspruch wahrzunehmen, und der Arbeitnehmer den Urlaub dennoch aus freien Stücken nicht genommen hat." Headnote 2 names the duty: to ask the employee to take the leave and to tell them clearly and in time that it lapses. https://www.bundesarbeitsgericht.de/entscheidung/9-azr-423-16/ (same date and subject: 9 AZR 541/15, https://www.bundesarbeitsgericht.de/entscheidung/9-azr-541-15/)
  - BAG 20 December 2022, 9 AZR 266/20, headnote 2: the three-year limitation period only starts once the employer has put the employee in a position to take the leave. https://www.bundesarbeitsgericht.de/entscheidung/9-azr-266-20/
  - BAG 20 December 2022, 9 AZR 245/19, Rn. 34: leave earned in a year in which the employee actually worked before becoming continuously unable to work „kann … grundsätzlich nur dann nach Ablauf eines Übertragungszeitraums von 15 Monaten erlöschen, wenn der Arbeitgeber den Arbeitnehmer durch Erfüllung seiner Mitwirkungsobliegenheiten rechtzeitig in die Lage versetzt hat, diesen Anspruch auszuüben." The decision also restates the 15-month rule for continued illness (BAG 7 August 2012, 9 AZR 353/10, cited there, not fetched separately). https://www.bundesarbeitsgericht.de/entscheidung/9-azr-245-19/
  - Contractual leave beyond the minimum: BAG 9 AZR 266/20, Rn. 25–26: the same principles apply to it; the parties may regulate it differently, but „müssen deutliche Anhaltspunkte vorliegen" for a will that it lapses regardless of the employer's notice.
- **Verified codebase gap**
  - Policy fields `allowCarryover` (default `false`), `maxCarryoverDays`, `carryoverExpiryMonths`: `apps/webapp/src/db/schema/vacation.ts:61-64`.
  - Year end: `apps/webapp/src/lib/absences/vacation-carryover.ts:225` sets the transferred days to 0 when the policy disallows carryover and otherwise applies the cap. Nothing separates statutory from contractual leave, and nothing asks whether the employee could take the leave.
  - Expiry: `carryoverExpiryInstant` (`vacation-carryover-terms.ts:7`) is a pure date; `expireCommittedVacationCarryover` (`vacation-carryover.ts:353`, expiry selection at `:425`) removes the days once the date has passed.
  - Notice to the employee: the admin report tells the admin „Please remind affected employees to use their carryover days before they expire" (`lib/reporting/vacation-reports.ts:607`); the balance card shows an "expiring soon" hint (`components/absences/vacation-balance-card.tsx:48`). A notification `onVacationBalanceAlert` exists (`lib/notifications/triggers.ts:1516`) but it has no production caller (only its definition and a test). No record is kept that a named employee was asked to take a stated number of days and warned of the lapse, and forfeiture does not depend on such a record.
  - Illness: no hit for `15 months|15 Monate|fifteen months` in source, messages or docs; `grep -iE "sick|illness"` in `db/schema/vacation.ts` returns nothing; `lib/absences/sick-vacation-override.ts` concerns sick days inside vacation, not expiry.
  - Other terms searched without result: `Mitwirkungsobliegenheit`, `Hinweispflicht`, `duty to notify`, `Urlaubsverfall`, `Verjähr`, `limitation period` (one unrelated hit in personnel-file retention comments).
- **Product impact:** With default settings Z8 transfers zero days at year end and debits carried-over days at the expiry date, without establishing that the employee had a real opportunity to take the leave and was asked and warned in time. For the statutory minimum (and for contractual leave unless it is clearly regulated differently) the leave has then not lapsed, so balances understate what the employer owes and no evidence exists when challenged. Required behaviour: (1) a dated, per-employee record that the notice was given (days open, request to take them, lapse date), whether Z8 sent it or the employer documents a notice given outside Z8; (2) entitlements whose lapse is not established stay visible as outstanding instead of being deleted; (3) illness: where the employee was continuously unable to work through the leave year and the carryover period, the leave lapses 15 months after the end of the leave year; for a year that was partly worked it depends on whether timely employer action could still have enabled the leave (BAG 9 AZR 245/19; BAG 31.01.2023, 9 AZR 107/20), so Z8 must not expire such leave automatically; (4) contractual leave above the minimum can follow its own terms where the policy says so.
- **Dedup:** searches `carryover`, `carryover expiry`, `Urlaubsverfall`, `Mitwirkungsobliegenheit`, `vacation expire sick`, `long-term illness vacation`, `Resturlaub`, `BUrlG`, `vacation expiry notify`. Closest: #1371 (assigned policies in accrual and carryover), #1104 and #990 (proration), ADR 0005 on frozen carryover terms. None deals with the conditions under which leave may lapse.
- **Confidence:** high on the law. Medium-high on the product reading: an organization can avoid wrong forfeiture today by allowing unlimited carryover without expiry and handling notices outside Z8.
- **Validation (Astra 6.2 High):** `REVISE`, applied. Law confirmed (CJEU C-684/16; BAG 9 AZR 423/16, 9 AZR 245/19, 9 AZR 107/20). Corrections: the notice is an Obliegenheit whose fulfilment may be evidenced outside Z8, so the requirement is to preserve unresolved entitlements and accept external evidence, not to make forfeiture depend on a Z8-sent notice; the illness rule distinguishes continuous incapacity from a partly worked year; code references corrected (`vacation-carryover.ts:225` caps the transfer, `:425` selects expiries; `onVacationBalanceAlert` has a test caller but no production caller). Dedup: #1371 and #1104 do not cover lapse conditions.

### F-03 — MuSchG §§ 3–6: scheduling and working-time checks do not reflect an employee's maternity protection restrictions

- **Classification:** Current gap
- **Legal basis and sources** (MuSchG as consolidated today, last amended by Art. 13 G v. 22.12.2025, BGBl. 2025 I Nr. 371)
  - § 3: no employment in the six weeks before birth unless she expressly agrees; none for eight weeks after (twelve for premature or multiple births or a diagnosed disability). § 3 Abs. 5, miscarriage: „bis zum Ablauf von zwei Wochen bei einer Fehlgeburt ab der 13. Schwangerschaftswoche oder … sechs Wochen … ab der 17. … oder … acht Wochen … ab der 20. Schwangerschaftswoche", unless she expressly agrees to work. https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/muschg_2018/__3.html
  - § 4: no work beyond 8.5 hours a day or 90 hours in a double week (under 18: 8 and 80), Sundays included; not beyond the contractual weekly hours on monthly average; 11 hours of rest. https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/muschg_2018/__4.html
  - § 5: no work between 20:00 and 06:00 (until 22:00 only under § 28). https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/muschg_2018/__5.html
  - § 6: no Sunday or public holiday work unless four cumulative conditions hold, including a compensatory rest day each week. https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/muschg_2018/__6.html
  - § 27 Abs. 3 and 5: records of type and extent of employment to be kept at least two years after the last entry. https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/muschg_2018/__27.html
  - Processing the pregnancy fact is permitted for these duties: § 26 Abs. 3 BDSG. https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bdsg_2018/__26.html
- **Verified codebase gap**
  - `grep -iE "MuSchG|Mutterschutz|maternity|pregnan|Schwanger"` over `apps/webapp/src`: four files, all labels only (`lib/payroll-export/types.ts:136` DATEV wage type name, `lib/clockodo/types.ts:88` import mapping, two payroll PDF files using a category name in tests). No hit in messages or docs for a rule.
  - No employee attribute for pregnancy or breastfeeding and no protection period: the statutory domains are `minijob`, `short_term`, `working_student`, `on_call`, `youth_protection`, `apprenticeship` (`lib/statutory/threshold-catalog.ts:8-15`).
  - Built-in absence types are vacation, sick, personal, home office, unpaid, parental, bereavement, time off in lieu (`lib/absences/default-absence-categories.ts`); none is maternity protection.
- **Product impact:** Z8 applies the adult policy (10 h days, night and Sunday work unrestricted) to a pregnant or breastfeeding employee and lets planners publish shifts inside a protection period. Z8 already overrides the adult policy for minors; this is the same pattern for a second protected group. Required, kept to what the scheduling decision needs: a restricted record of the effective protection (period in which no work may be planned, and the §§ 4–6 limits that apply), entered by an authorized person; planners see only that a restriction applies, never a diagnosis, the week of a miscarriage or the reason. The record must be able to express: the six weeks before birth and the employee's revocable willingness to work in them (§ 3 Abs. 1); eight or twelve weeks after birth, extended by the days lost through an early delivery, and the disability-related extension only on the employee's request (§ 3 Abs. 2); the graduated miscarriage periods of two, six or eight weeks from the 13th, 17th or 20th week, again subject to her express, revocable willingness (§ 3 Abs. 5, in force since 2025-06-01); the § 4 limits including the double week and the contractual monthly average; the §§ 5–6 bans with their exceptions and authorizations (§§ 28–29). Findings warn in scheduling and at clock-out. Recording of work actually done is never blocked. § 27 Abs. 5 MuSchG (two years from the last entry) concerns the employer's specified documents and sets a minimum for that evidence.
- **Dedup:** searches `Mutterschutz`, `maternity`, `MuSchG`, `pregnant`, `breastfeeding`, `Beschäftigungsverbot`: no issue or PR.
- **Confidence:** high on the law; medium-high on scope. It needs a health-related data point, so the design must settle access and retention first.
- **Validation (Astra 6.2 High):** `REVISE`, applied. Law confirmed (MuSchG as consolidated; miscarriage periods by the amendment in BGBl. 2025 I Nr. 59, effective 2025-06-01; § 26 Abs. 3 BDSG). Corrections: narrowed to protection-aware planning and checks; data collection limited to the effective restriction instead of health details visible to admins; exceptions (§ 3 willingness, early delivery, request-based extension, §§ 28–29) stated correctly; § 27 Abs. 5 scope corrected. Dedup: none (#1993 covers youth and apprentices only).

### F-04 — ArbZG § 11: adult scheduling has no check of free Sundays or compensatory rest days

- **Classification:** Current gap
- **Legal basis and sources** (ArbZG as consolidated today, last amended by Art. 52 G v. 23.10.2024, BGBl. 2024 I Nr. 323)
  - § 9 Abs. 1: no employment on Sundays and public holidays from 0 to 24 h (exceptions in § 10). https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/arbzg/__9.html
  - § 11 Abs. 1: „Mindestens 15 Sonntage im Jahr müssen beschäftigungsfrei bleiben." Abs. 3: after Sunday work an „Ersatzruhetag … innerhalb eines den Beschäftigungstag einschließenden Zeitraums von zwei Wochen"; after work on a public holiday falling on a working day, within eight weeks. Abs. 2: Sunday and holiday hours count toward the §§ 3, 6 Abs. 2 maxima. https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/arbzg/__11.html
  - Pending change, not yet law: the government draft of 18 November 2026 rewrites § 11 Abs. 1 as an employer duty („Der Arbeitgeber hat dem Arbeitnehmer mindestens 15 beschäftigungsfreie Sonntage im Jahr zu gewähren") and widens § 10. See W-2.
- **Verified codebase gap**
  - Schedule compliance produces four finding types, `restTime`, `maxHours`, `overtime`, `statutory` (`lib/scheduling/compliance/types.ts:5`); the regulation table has no Sunday or holiday field (`db/schema/work-policy.ts:312-358`).
  - The violation enum has Sunday and holiday entries only for minors (`db/schema/enums.ts:412-414`, `jarbschg_*`).
  - `grep -iE "Ersatzruhetag|compensatory.?rest|§ ?11 ArbZG|§ ?9 ArbZG"` over source, messages and docs: no hit. `grep -iE "sunday|sonntag"` in `lib` outside youth, calendar and date helpers: surcharge validation, coverage rules, statutory week helpers; no adult rest rule.
- **Product impact:** Customers who lawfully work Sundays or holidays (hospitality, care, retail exceptions, shift operations) get no warning when an employee can no longer reach the required free Sundays in the year or when no compensatory rest day falls inside the statutory window. Required: count worked and free Sundays per employee and calendar year against the applicable minimum (15 by statute; 10, 8 or 6 where a collective arrangement under § 12 applies, so the value is configurable); for each worked Sunday find a work-free day within two weeks, and for each worked public holiday that falls on a Werktag (Saturday included) within eight weeks, each window including the day worked; treat the replacement day as directly connected to a § 5 rest period unless the organization documents technical or organizational reasons (§ 11 Abs. 4); show both in scheduling before publishing, as a provisional result while the roster for the window is still incomplete.
- **Dedup:** searches `Sunday holiday work`, `Ersatzruhetag`, `Sonntagsarbeit`, `compensatory rest day`, `Sunday`: only the JArbSchG work (#1993, #1934) and unrelated week-start bugs (#994).
- **Confidence:** high on the law; medium-high on scope. Whether Sunday work is permitted at all (§ 10, permits) stays outside Z8.
- **Validation (Astra 6.2 High):** `REVISE`, applied. Law confirmed (§ 11 Abs. 1, 3, 4 and § 12 ArbZG). Corrections: the Sunday minimum and holiday compensation can be changed by collective arrangement (§ 12); § 11 Abs. 4 link to the daily rest period added; holiday rule applies to holidays on a Werktag including Saturday; results are provisional for incomplete rosters. Code check confirmed: the schedule evaluator checks rest intervals only and Sunday/holiday rules exist only in the JArbSchG rules. Dedup: #1934, #1993 (youth), #994 (week boundaries) do not cover it.

### F-05 — ArbZG § 3 Satz 2 / § 6 Abs. 2: the German preset flags a single week above 48 h as a violation, and no averaging over the compensation period exists

- **Classification:** Rule drift / edge case
- **Legal basis and sources**
  - § 3: „Sie kann auf bis zu zehn Stunden nur verlängert werden, wenn innerhalb von sechs Kalendermonaten oder innerhalb von 24 Wochen im Durchschnitt acht Stunden werktäglich nicht überschritten werden." https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/arbzg/__3.html
  - § 6 Abs. 2: for night workers the average must hold within one calendar month or four weeks. https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/arbzg/__6.html
  - § 16 Abs. 2: hours beyond eight per working day must be recorded and kept two years. https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/arbzg/__16.html
- **Verified codebase gap**
  - The German preset stores `maxDailyMinutes: 600` and `maxWeeklyMinutes: 2880` with the description „max 48h/week" (`db/seed/work-policy-presets.ts:123-132`).

# Webapp UI/UX refresh: domain and access constraints

Status: factual discovery and recommendations for [#1692](https://github.com/Umami-Creative-GmbH/z8/issues/1692), 14 November 2026. This supports the shared specification and route tickets; it does not approve changes to domain rules, permissions, feature activation or deployment. Scope is user-facing route templates in `apps/webapp`, including authentication, setup, shared views, settings and platform administration. API implementations and the other apps are outside the refactor scope, but their contracts constrain the web UI.

The [shared UI and UX language](../refs/ux-language.md) is the presentation baseline. Existing capabilities, preferences and authoritative calculations remain requirements. The [refresh verification](2026-11-14-refresh-verification.md) establishes useful evidence for dashboard, time tracking and the shared app shell, with explicit unverified paths. It is not acceptance evidence for every other route.

## Common contract for every route ticket

Each ticket should identify the organization, person, period and authority behind the page; map its current reads, mutations, detail/download routes, filters, shortcuts and saved preferences to the new composition; and list the state/permission combinations to verify. A shared visual component must not become a shared authorization or calculation rule merely because two screens look similar.

## Access and privacy boundaries

### Settings, workforce and platform administration

Settings actions require one of four ordered **settings access** levels: org member, scoped manager, org admin or owner. Approved membership is required; a former employee has no settings access even if their membership says owner. An owner/admin membership can grant org-admin access without an employee record. An employee with the admin employee role but no owner/admin membership is a **workforce admin**, a scoped manager over the whole organization. Account settings such as password, picture and name belong to no organization. The older three-value sidebar `SettingsAccessTier` is still distinct from the four action levels. Preserve the existing resolver and page/control checks instead of rebuilding visibility from a role label. [Settings ADR](../adr/0002-settings-actions-authorize-by-settings-access-not-casl.md), [project conventions](../refs/project-conventions.md#rbac), [Organization glossary](../../apps/webapp/src/lib/organization/CONTEXT.md#organization-access).

CASL continues to govern object-level checks inside settings handlers and authorization outside settings. Platform administration is a separate system role; its shell and actions have their own guards. A platform-admin session does not by itself confer the organization decision right used by time approvals. Reuse the established authorization paths and make platform-wide targets explicit on privileged action forms. [Settings ADR](../adr/0002-settings-actions-authorize-by-settings-access-not-casl.md), [platform-admin layout](../../apps/webapp/src/app/[locale]/(admin)/admin-layout-content.tsx), [platform-admin service](../../apps/webapp/src/lib/effect/services/platform-admin.service.ts), [decision-right ADR](../../apps/webapp/src/lib/approvals/docs/adr/0003-a-decision-is-made-under-one-claimed-decision-right.md).

## Time, calculations and evidence

### Date and timezone meanings

| Value or view | Authoritative interpretation to preserve |
| --- | --- |
| Time entry / work-period endpoints | UTC instants are canonical; duration is their elapsed UTC difference. Each endpoint keeps its own captured offset and supporting IANA zone/source. Travel/DST can give the endpoints different offsets. Do not re-label both using the viewer's zone or subtract displayed wall times. [Timekeeping](../refs/timekeeping.md) |
| Self-service manual time | Interpret wall-clock values in the timezone the form names. The mismatch choices update saved timezone, continue once in browser timezone, or cancel; continuing once must also parse using that effective zone. [Timekeeping](../refs/timekeeping.md#manual-entries) |
| On-behalf time / employee calendar | Use the selected employee's timezone, including local query boundaries and day keys. Never substitute the manager's browser zone. [Timekeeping](../refs/timekeeping.md#calendar-display-and-query-boundaries) |
| Day total vs compliance total | Day total splits completed/live work at employee-local midnight; compliance counts a period whole on its start day. These can legitimately differ. Recorded violations use the policy effective at work end and are never re-evaluated later. [Time Tracking](../../apps/webapp/src/lib/time-tracking/CONTEXT.md), [policy-evidence ADR](../../apps/webapp/src/lib/time-tracking/docs/adr/0003-work-policies-are-captured-evidence-not-organization-configuration.md) |
| Compliance week | Monday midnight to next Monday midnight, independent of personal calendar week-start and organization submission cadence. Scheduling uses the organization's calendar; recorded-work compliance keeps its own employee-time aggregation. [Compliance-week ADR](../../apps/webapp/src/lib/time-tracking/docs/adr/0012-compliance-weeks-start-on-monday.md) |
| Submission period | Employee-local week/calendar month, clipped to employment and frozen as a range when submitted. A later timezone change does not move it. [Time Tracking](../../apps/webapp/src/lib/time-tracking/CONTEXT.md#period-submissions) |
| Shift date / skill expiry / request deadline | Shift date is organization-local, not a UTC or viewer-local day. Skill expiry is a plain organization-calendar day and stays valid on that day. A document request becomes overdue after its deadline on the organization's calendar. [Scheduling](../../apps/webapp/src/lib/scheduling/CONTEXT.md), [Personnel File](../../apps/webapp/src/lib/personnel-file/CONTEXT.md) |
| Expense/document dates | Expense date and document date are plain calendar dates. A trip has its own timezone; a standalone report does not. Per-diem departure and return can each have their own zone. Preserve the date type instead of applying one global timezone formatter. [Travel Expenses](../../apps/webapp/src/lib/travel-expenses/CONTEXT.md#dates), [Personnel File](../../apps/webapp/src/lib/personnel-file/CONTEXT.md#documents) |

New or migrated date/time business logic uses Temporal with explicit zones. Native `Date` is a boundary type, and existing Luxon code is legacy. Presentation work should continue reading domain calculations; moving a value into a new summary is not a reason to calculate a client-side substitute. [Date/time reference](../refs/dates.md), [timekeeping reference](../refs/timekeeping.md).

### Freezing and correction are domain-specific

| Record or workflow | Preserve this behavior |
| --- | --- |
| Closed month | Freezes covered employees' whole employee-local months as fixed at close, including cross-boundary records and attribution, absences and balance adjustments. Later team/timezone changes do not move it. Authorized reopening with a reason is the route to change it; notes are not frozen. A payroll export and a closed month are independent. [Closed-month ADR](../../apps/webapp/src/lib/time-tracking/docs/adr/0004-closed-months-are-fixed-at-close-and-refused-by-the-database.md) |
| Period submission | Confirms records; it does not freeze them. A substantive later change sends the period back for submission: pending approval is withdrawn, approved approval stays in history but goes out of date. Notes do not count. Keep this distinct from rejection and reopening. [Time Tracking](../../apps/webapp/src/lib/time-tracking/CONTEXT.md#period-submissions) |
| Change policy | Direct-edit and approval windows use calendar days in the employee's zone. Older work may be ineligible for an employee's edit but still recordable as a manual entry requiring approval. The rule differs for on-behalf changes and org owners/admins changing their own work. Preserve returned action eligibility. [Time Tracking](../../apps/webapp/src/lib/time-tracking/CONTEXT.md#language) |
| Work balance | The shared projection combines completed work, required time and the adjustment ledger. An opening balance replaces earlier accumulated time through its date; a mistaken adjustment is cancelled with actor/reason and stays visible. It is not a free-form editable total. [Balance-adjustment ADR](../../apps/webapp/src/lib/time-tracking/docs/adr/0008-balance-adjustments-are-a-cancellable-ledger-and-an-opening-balance-replaces-earlier-time.md) |
| Absence days / vacation | Absence days resolve per day from the effective schedule/holiday, with half days counting one half regardless of scheduled hours. Policy gaps/conflicts require review. Stored accrual and carryover outcomes retain committed amounts/expiry; routine policy changes or retries do not rewrite them. [Absence-day ADR](../../apps/webapp/src/lib/absences/docs/adr/0001-absence-days-resolve-at-read-from-the-work-policy-in-effect-on-each-day.md), [postings ADR](../../apps/webapp/src/lib/absences/docs/adr/0005-vacation-postings-preserve-committed-amounts-and-expiry.md) |
| Employment terms / pricing | Confirmed employment terms are the wage history; proposals are not effective. Wage, billable rate and internal cost rate are distinct. Uninvoiced billable work resolves effective rates when read; invoice drafts freeze their hand-off rates. Missing price is not zero and missing cost makes margin unknown. [Wage ADR](../../apps/webapp/src/lib/organization/docs/adr/0002-employment-terms-are-the-only-wage-history.md), [rates ADR](../../apps/webapp/src/lib/billable-time/docs/adr/0001-rates-resolve-at-read-and-freeze-at-hand-off.md), [Billable Time](../../apps/webapp/src/lib/billable-time/CONTEXT.md) |
| Invoiced work | Z8 hands off invoice drafts, never issues invoices. Invoicing does not block truthful corrections. Changes leave a changed-after-invoicing mark requiring a reasoned admin resolution; comparison uses the frozen rate and may be unknown. Releasing a draft retains history. [Hand-off ADR](../../apps/webapp/src/lib/billable-time/docs/adr/0002-hand-off-drafts-never-issue-invoices.md), [context](../../apps/webapp/src/lib/billable-time/CONTEXT.md#hand-off) |
| Inspection/equipment evidence | A driving check is supplemented, not overwritten, and has its own retention. Equipment receipt, loss, waiver and recovery are different outcomes; recovery supplements the original and does not settle a quantity twice. [Driving-evidence ADR](../../apps/webapp/src/lib/organization/docs/adr/0004-driving-authorization-and-check-evidence-have-their-own-lifecycle.md), [equipment reference](../refs/equipment-return-tracking.md) |
| Payroll comparison | Historical evidence represents actual exported output independently of current records or expired download files. A current preview reads without delivering, repairing history or changing a run/closed month. Supported line kinds and excluded expense reimbursements remain explicit. [Payroll evidence ADR](../../apps/webapp/src/lib/payroll-export/docs/adr/0001-comparison-evidence-preserves-exported-output.md) |

## Operational workflows and recovery

**Approvals.** Keep requester, subject, submitter, current approver and actual deciding actor distinct. A decision claims one right and rechecks that right in its transaction; a stale right does not silently fall back to another the actor holds. A deputy covers without taking over the assignment, and history records both people. An exact-item link is navigation, not authority: arrival rechecks organization, membership and current read entitlement. Missing, purged, reassigned and forbidden items intentionally share an unavailable outcome; infrastructure failure remains an error. [Approvals context](../../apps/webapp/src/lib/approvals/CONTEXT.md), [decision-right ADR](../../apps/webapp/src/lib/approvals/docs/adr/0003-a-decision-is-made-under-one-claimed-decision-right.md), [review arrival](../../apps/webapp/src/lib/approvals/presentation/review-arrival.ts).

Legacy/canonical approval authority, evidence capture, delivery admission and Time Tracking admission are separate rollouts. Preserve held/material-change/evidence-unavailable states and existing supported recovery. A modern-looking queue must not enable a decision that the authority currently holds or introduce a new lifecycle transition. [Context map](../../GLOSSARY-MAP.md), [Approvals context](../../apps/webapp/src/lib/approvals/CONTEXT.md), [approval evidence](../refs/approval-evidence.md).

**Absence changes.** An employee's change/cancellation of an approved absence remains a proposal until another eligible approver approves it. Keep original and proposed dates/coverage, required reason and withdrawal history. The proposed dates do not become a second effective absence or reserve extra vacation. Approval revalidates and can mark a stale proposal obsolete. Approved full cancellation deletes linked sick notes; rejection/withdrawal does not. Shortening/cancelling sickness does not restore displaced vacation automatically. These are domain decisions to retain wherever the currently implemented workflow exposes them. [Absence-change ADR](../../apps/webapp/src/lib/absences/docs/adr/0002-recorded-absence-changes-require-another-approvers-approval.md).

**Clocking.** Header, dashboard, time page and presence views must agree about working, break-in-progress and stopped states. A break in progress is server state; ending the day during it ends work at the break's start. Device inactivity alone does not establish a break. Vehicle details and consent-dependent position capture cannot block/delay a clock command. Vehicle use is amendable attribution; a position stamp is immutable event evidence, optional, consent-bound and deleted on withdrawal. No refactor adds background/geofence tracking. [Time Tracking context](../../apps/webapp/src/lib/time-tracking/CONTEXT.md#clocking), [break ADR](../../apps/webapp/src/lib/time-tracking/docs/adr/0007-a-break-in-progress-is-recorded-on-the-server.md), [vehicle ADR](../../apps/webapp/src/lib/time-tracking/docs/adr/0009-vehicle-use-is-amendable-work-attribution-not-evidence.md), [position ADR](../../apps/webapp/src/lib/time-tracking/docs/adr/0004-position-stamps-require-position-consent.md), [thin-shell ADR](../adr/0001-store-apps-are-a-thin-shell-around-the-web-app.md).

Task requirements have a different rule: a present actor who can choose a task is refused without one; unattended/replayed writers are accepted and marked as missing a task. A person unable to book the retained project cannot pick a task and must still be able to end live work. Preserve these differences when simplifying clock-out controls. Projects remain flat, tasks are the sole level below, and templates are not bookable projects. [Task-requirement ADR](../../apps/webapp/src/lib/projects/docs/adr/0002-a-task-requirement-refuses-only-writers-that-can-pick-a-task.md), [project-structure ADR](../../apps/webapp/src/lib/projects/docs/adr/0001-projects-are-flat-tasks-are-the-only-level-below.md).

**Offline and uncertain outcomes.** Frozen clock commands preserve the event's device timezone at capture and are accepted only for adopted organizations. Queued, confirmed, refused and uncertain outcomes must stay distinct. Online kiosk response loss requires lookup of the same operation before offering another action; lookup must not silently execute an uncommitted action. Offline kiosk PIN commands are explicitly unconfirmed until Z8 checks the current PIN on arrival; retryable throttling keeps the same command. Badge clocking is online-only proof; a fob only selects the employee and still needs their PIN. Do not merge these flows into a generic optimistic success toast. [Timekeeping](../refs/timekeeping.md#offline-and-mobile-sync), [admission ADR](../../apps/webapp/src/lib/time-tracking/docs/adr/0002-clocking-owns-both-admissions.md), [offline PIN ADR](../../apps/webapp/src/lib/time-tracking/docs/adr/0011-an-offline-kiosk-pin-is-checked-only-when-it-reaches-z8.md), [badge ADR](../../apps/webapp/src/lib/time-tracking/docs/adr/0013-kiosk-badges-authorize-online-clocking-without-a-pin.md), [fob ADR](../../apps/webapp/src/lib/time-tracking/docs/adr/0014-a-kiosk-fob-selects-the-employee-and-the-kiosk-pin-still-proves.md).

**Expenses and payroll.** Draft reports may contain incomplete/future plans; submission requires nothing still needed. Export moves no money. Payroll inclusion does not mean reimbursement, is hidden from the employee until reimbursed, and can prevent another reimbursement while included. Only an authorized expense officer confirms payment, and only within scope; payroll access alone does not grant that power. Recovery stays outside payroll. Late receipt documents supplement frozen submissions with supplier/time and do not rewrite earlier exports. Preserve per-record connector outcomes and warnings rather than turning mixed results into a single success. Withdrawn connectors keep history but are absent from new setup/delivery choices. [Travel Expenses](../../apps/webapp/src/lib/travel-expenses/CONTEXT.md), [payroll confirmation ADR](../../apps/webapp/src/lib/travel-expenses/docs/adr/0003-payroll-runs-record-reimbursements-only-on-officer-confirmation.md), [late-receipt ADR](../../apps/webapp/src/lib/travel-expenses/docs/adr/0005-late-receipts-supplement-frozen-submissions.md), [connector withdrawal ADR](../../apps/webapp/src/lib/payroll-export/docs/adr/0002-connectors-ship-on-documented-apis-or-are-withdrawn.md), [Personio ADR](../../apps/webapp/src/lib/payroll-export/docs/adr/0003-personio-delivers-through-v2-and-tracks-delivered-records.md).

**Documents, imports and setup review.** Document-request uploads enter the personnel file immediately; returning/cancelling a request does not remove them, and only acceptance renews the linked skill/document. Payslip batches need confirmed employee matches. Reviewed imports distinguish scanning, needs-review, committing, completed, failed and cancelled states; a blocking issue cannot be accepted into a committable row merely by changing its display status. Go-live checks are fresh reads; rehearsal changes nothing, notifies nobody and delivers nothing. Preserve review steps rather than recasting them as completed background actions. [Document-request ADR](../../apps/webapp/src/lib/personnel-file/docs/adr/0003-requested-documents-enter-the-file-at-once.md), [Personnel File](../../apps/webapp/src/lib/personnel-file/CONTEXT.md), [import states](../../apps/webapp/src/lib/import-review/state.ts), [import types](../../apps/webapp/src/lib/import-review/types.ts), [go-live ADR](../../apps/webapp/src/lib/organization/docs/adr/0007-going-live-is-checked-never-staged.md).

**Scheduling.** Planned shifts remain distinct from recorded work. Open and published/draft are independent properties. Staffing suggestions are advisory, never applied automatically; blockers rule a person out while warnings may be knowingly accepted. A missing/expired required skill needs a reasoned skill override on assignment, unlike a preferred skill. A dated skill certificate can make the skill expiry date read-only on the skill side. Preserve picker eligibility, warnings, explicit planner choice and approval of pickup/swap requests. [Scheduling context](../../apps/webapp/src/lib/scheduling/CONTEXT.md), [skill-certificate ADR](../../apps/webapp/src/lib/scheduling/docs/adr/0001-skill-certificates-set-the-skill-expiry-date.md).

**Employment ending.** Current-roster views exclude former employees while historical views retain and mark them. At a departure cutoff, access/seat ending is already effective even when persisted follow-up tasks are pending or failed. Missing equipment never delays it. Rehire is a new employment period; reactivation undoes deactivation but restores none of the holdings that ended. Keep consequential previews, replacement checks, history, persisted follow-up counts, retry/review actions and the admin reactivation checklist. Do not label unfinished follow-up as continued employment or imply equipment receipt restores a credential. [Deactivation ADR](../../apps/webapp/src/lib/organization/docs/adr/0006-deactivation-makes-a-former-employee-like-a-departure.md), [member-removal ADR](../../apps/webapp/src/lib/organization/docs/adr/0011-member-removal-and-scim-deprovisioning-are-deactivations.md), [equipment-obligation ADR](../../apps/webapp/src/lib/organization/docs/adr/0008-equipment-return-obligations-outlive-employment.md), [offboarding reference](../refs/employee-offboarding.md).

## Billing, optional features and notifications

Billing has disabled, trialing, active and suspended access states, with distinct subscription-required, trial-expired, payment-failed and canceled reasons. `BILLING_ENABLED` can disable the subsystem. The authenticated shell permits billing recovery routes during suspension; its failure to check billing also fails closed. Preserve recovery access and role-specific guidance. The billing evaluator remains authoritative; a count of days or a differently styled banner must not decide entitlement. [Billing reference](../refs/billing-stripe.md), [access evaluator](../../apps/webapp/src/lib/effect/services/billing/billing-access.ts), [app gate](../../apps/webapp/src/app/[locale]/(app)/app-layout-content.tsx), [suspended page](../../apps/webapp/src/app/[locale]/(app)/billing/suspended/page.tsx).

The Z8 subscription is distinct from Billable Time, which concerns an organization charging its own customers. Full and kiosk seats share the billable-seat definition; a kiosk seat split exists only when both kiosk prices are configured. Former employees do not count even while offboarding's release gate is closed. The public `/licenses` page is an open-source license report, not a subscription/feature-license editor. No separate commercial licensing model was identified in this discovery; do not invent one for navigation or empty states. [Context map](../../GLOSSARY-MAP.md), [billing reference](../refs/billing-stripe.md), [licenses page](../../apps/webapp/src/app/[locale]/(auth)/licenses/page.tsx).

Optional feature switches, assignment inheritance and deployment release gates are separate from access permission. For example, switching driving licence control off hides the feature and stops reminders while deadlines/evidence cleanup persist; equipment off preserves records/outstanding obligations and retention processing. An employee assignment can override primary-team and organization defaults, including explicit off. The offboarding command release gate does not undo existing effective access/seat ending, and review/retry actions remain available. Route tickets must preserve the observed enabled/disabled/unconfigured/restricted states and cannot activate features as a side effect of restyling. [Organization context](../../apps/webapp/src/lib/organization/CONTEXT.md), [intended-driver ADR](../../apps/webapp/src/lib/organization/docs/adr/0005-intended-driver-is-a-scoped-assignment-and-losing-it-ends-authorization.md), [equipment implementation reference](../refs/equipment-return-tracking.md), [offboarding release gate](../refs/employee-offboarding.md#release-gate-and-rollback).

Notifications honor enabled channels and preferences. Recipient language differs from the page viewer's locale; organization email overrides are intentionally sent as written in one language. Preserve channel-specific settings, template variables and the editor's warning about single-language overrides. A new visual form must not silently discard existing overrides. Scheduled export ownership is also operational: the schedule pauses when its owner ceases to be an org admin. [Notifications context](../../apps/webapp/src/lib/notifications/CONTEXT.md), [email ADR](../../apps/webapp/src/lib/notifications/docs/adr/0001-notification-emails-use-one-localized-layout-and-overrides-are-sent-as-written.md), [Scheduled Exports](../../apps/webapp/src/lib/scheduled-exports/CONTEXT.md).

Organization display name, legal company name, registered address, operating locations and Stripe details are distinct data. AVV downloads use the current organization profile; they are not versioned acceptance/signature evidence. Preserve current-profile validation and authorized download behavior without suggesting the PDF proves which details were accepted historically. [Organization identity](../../apps/webapp/src/lib/organization/CONTEXT.md#organization-identity), [AVV ADR](../../apps/webapp/src/lib/organization/docs/adr/0009-avv-uses-current-organization-profile.md), [profile reference](../refs/organization-profile.md).

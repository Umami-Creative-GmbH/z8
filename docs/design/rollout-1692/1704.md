## Design intent

A workforce manager compares recorded hours across teams, then inspects count/average/status and employee variance. The page should feel precise and calm. Domain vocabulary: team, recorded hours, employee count, average, variance, organization period. Use existing blue primary/chart tokens, neutral card/background/border and semantic failure tokens, existing app font and 4px spacing scale. Fine bordered sections keep a wide operational record readable. The signature is the aligned team/total/average/count/status comparison with pinned team identity. Keep controls in one toolbar, the comparison and full record first, variance second; avoid decorative metric tiles, independent per-number cards and hidden phone columns. No rebrand or font changes.

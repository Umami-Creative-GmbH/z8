## Design intent

An operations manager compares recorded work with expected hours over an organization period, then checks employee variance and weekly averages. The direction is a restrained operational report: existing app font, blue primary/chart tokens, neutral background/borders and semantic warning/destructive tokens. Domain anchors are recorded hours, expected hours, variance, employee, weekly average and organization period. The aligned employee/actual/expected/variance/status record is the signature; daily/variance evidence shares a row on wide displays and stacks first on phones. Controls lead; the employee record and distribution follow. Avoid decorative metric tiles, nested per-value cards and hidden phone columns. DFII: impact3 + fit5 + feasibility5 + performance4 − consistency-risk2 = 15. Canonical product constraints override generic skill suggestions for new fonts/palettes.

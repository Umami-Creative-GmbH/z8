## Inherited domain limitations — coordinated before authority changes

The existing `OnboardingService.setWorkSchedule(_data)` ignores hours,
classification and effectiveFrom: schedules are template-owned and inherited from
the organization default. Save and skip only persist `userSettings.onboardingStep`
according to membership role. Neither establishes a new work policy or changes
contractual hours. The service checks membership to choose a destination, but does
not refuse a non-admin schedule submission. `isUserAdmin` checks owner/admin role
without the settings-access approval/current-roster policy. These are inherited
server/domain authorization and persistence limitations, reported to root; this
presentation ticket does not change that authority or claim a saved policy.

The UI retains the existing payload and effective boundary. Acceptance of actual
policy/hour persistence and server-side non-admin refusal remains unpassed unless
the owner is separately changed and verified.

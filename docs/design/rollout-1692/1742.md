## Design intent

Managers inspect expected employees, cadence periods, employee-local ranges, confirmation state and resubmission needs before visiting Calendar or Inbox. Use the established app font, neutral canvas/card/border/foreground tokens, primary blue for navigation and destructive/amber status cues with text redundancy. Period control plus running context is the signature; a single count strip replaces separate metric cards, records replace decorative summaries, and full labels replace phone icon-only actions. Four-unit spacing and border-led depth preserve the established operational language. No new font/palette or persistence owner.

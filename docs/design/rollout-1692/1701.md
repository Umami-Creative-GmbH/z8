- F1 `PageHeader` owns /absences atomically, including no-employee and loading arrivals; the app error boundary derives the same ownership. Exact opt-in preserves other route headings. A compact shared metric surface replaces nested cards, with localized numerals and units. Missing policy, conflicting policies and zero-working-day setup get their original distinct explanation without hiding independent authorized records or bypassing request validation.
- F2 pagination composes with the table's existing local state; stable IDs and stacked phone rows expose all fetched history and every field/action. Row actions wrap at large text. Search execution, request/approved-change/sick-note/deputy actions and domain feedback remain unchanged.
- Calendar/table controls retain a single consumer-owned request panel. Year loading/failure identifies the last loaded year and allows retry; failed reads cannot relabel old data. Late current-year reads cannot overwrite newer RSC snapshots. The employee/organization identity keys the consumer boundary so another scope does not inherit its data or draft; ordinary refresh keeps the same identity and draft lifetime.
- Request, change, deputy and sick-note forms were not edited. Their TanStack forms, F3 ActionPanel, typed payloads, eligibility, approval rules, upload grants and cancellation behavior remain authoritative. No action, schema, permission or calculation implementation changed.

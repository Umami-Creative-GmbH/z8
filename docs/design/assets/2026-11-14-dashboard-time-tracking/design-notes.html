My recommendation is to take this layout into a working implementation review. The blue palette, German vocabulary and operational emphasis come from Z8. The exact spacing, card proportions, grouped navigation, initials in place of photo assets and collapsed utility widgets are design judgments, rather than copies of a reference.

What to try

Use the bottom bar or sidebar to switch screens. Choose a workplace and start or stop the demo clock. Select 11, 12 or 13 November to view the recorded day. Switch between list and calendar, expand approved requests and optional widgets, and toggle the moon button for dark mode. Other destination controls explain the existing view they would open.

Open prototype.html directly in a browser. It needs no server or installation. All sample state resets on reload. The prototype has no backend connection, live clock, persistence, authentication, or working request and correction forms.

Data details for an implementation

The dashboard screenshot uses a monthly target of 171.4 h; time tracking shows 168 h. Each view retains its source figure. The 11 November entry from 09:08 to 12:45 displays 3h 36m in the screenshot; that duration is retained. The zero-duration 12 November entry also remains visible. Daily totals sum the displayed fixture durations; they are not an authoritative payroll calculation.

Production durations must come from canonical UTC records, with the captured event-local offsets kept at each endpoint. This prototype uses fixed visual fixtures and does not change timekeeping logic. Monday and Tuesday show a dash because their complete records were not provided.

Verification

Browser checks cover both view links, workplace selection, synchronized demo clock state, day selection, list/calendar mode, dark mode, mobile navigation, keyboard focus and horizontal overflow. Desktop and phone screenshots were inspected. React-specific performance and composition rules do not apply to this plain HTML study.
